BetterKick brings Kick VODs and live streams to the next level with one‑click downloads, stream recording, and powerful moderator tools.
Everything runs inside Kick with no external services or tracking.

BetterKick is the ultimate extension for everyone who lives on Kick—viewers, creators, and moderators alike. Download full VODs, save only the moments you care about, record live streams in real time, and level up your moderation workflow without ever leaving Kick.

Features for everyone

Download full VODs directly from the stream or VOD page with no external services or extra steps. If you do not need the whole video, pick a start and end time to save only the segment you want—perfect for recaps, highlights, or long clips.

When you follow or unfollow someone on Kick, BetterKick adds visible animations that make the action much clearer and more noticeable, giving the platform a more lively feel.

If the stream volume is too low even at max volume, BetterKick includes a built‑in audio amplifier with up to +48 dB of gain. It is ideal for quiet streams, as long as you use it responsibly to avoid distortion or damage to your ears or speakers.

Features for moderators

BetterKick is also built for people who moderate Kick channels. You can record the live stream while it is happening and save the result on your device, without depending on the VOD being available later.

The extension automatically highlights the first message from any user in chat, using an internal database to know when it is their first time. This helps you instantly spot new people joining the channel (it may take a couple of days to fully learn recurring users).

You can also configure keyword highlights to flag specific messages—ideal for catching bots, trolls, or any user you want to identify at a glance without reading every single line.

BetterKick includes a moderation command library. Save frequent commands (for example, for server bots) and send them to chat with a single click, saving time and avoiding typos under pressure.

Finally, you can pin messages temporarily: when you pin a message, you choose how long it stays highlighted before it clears automatically, keeping the chat organized without manual cleanup.

Privacy and data usage

BetterKick is designed with a clear principle: no personal or usage data collection. The extension does not store your browsing history, chat messages, account identifiers, or any telemetry.

All configuration you use—such as keywords or command library entries—is stored only in your browser so the extension can function properly. None of this is sent to external servers.

Most importantly, we do not collect data to sell, analyze, or share with third parties. VOD downloads and live stream recordings always happen directly from Kick’s servers to your browser. BetterKick never uses intermediate servers to process video or audio.

Compatibility

BetterKick is built specifically for Kick.com and its related domains. It works on Chromium‑based browsers and Firefox, respecting each browser’s extension limitations.

If you find a bug, notice strange behavior, or have ideas for new features, you can contact the developer or open an issue in the project repository. Many improvements and fixes can be shipped quickly in new BetterKick versions.
